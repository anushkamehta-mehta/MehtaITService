About us

Data protection and privacy compliance advisory for technology teams.

Who we are

Mehta Mehta advises organisations on data protection and privacy compliance. We work closely with IT, engineering, product and security teams so that legal requirements become practical tasks they can plan and deliver.

Who we help

CTOs, CISOs, heads of engineering, product managers, data protection officers and IT managers in any organisation that handles personal data.

How we work

We start by understanding how your systems and data flows actually work. We then explain the legal requirements in plain terms, write the policies and workflows, and stay available as your products and vendors change.

What we do not do

We provide legal and compliance advice. We do not build software, host systems or carry out technical security testing. Your IT team stays in control of your systems, and we make sure they have clear requirements to work from.
