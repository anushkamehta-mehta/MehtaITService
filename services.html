Our services

Data Protection & Privacy Compliance Advisory for IT and technology teams.

Compliance your IT team can act on

The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 affects how systems store, move and delete personal data. We handle the legal and process side and give your IT team clear requirements. Each service below can be used on its own.

Data Mapping

We build an inventory of the personal data across your systems, vendors and business functions, together with a record of how it is processed.

Where your IT team comes in: IT teams know where data is stored and which systems and vendors touch it, so they are the main source for this work.

Ask about data mapping

Consent Flows & Notices

We design consent flows and user notices that product, legal and customer-facing teams can use.

Where your IT team comes in: product and engineering teams build these flows and notices into your apps and websites.

Ask about consent flows

Rights & Grievance Workflows

We design how requests for access, correction and erasure, and user grievances, are handled, with set turnaround times.

Where your IT team comes in: IT needs to be able to find, change or delete a person's data when a request comes in.

Ask about rights workflows

Breach Response Playbook & Drills

We write the escalation paths, notification templates and Board-reporting triggers, and run breach-readiness drills.

Where your IT team comes in: IT or security teams are normally the first to detect a breach and must follow this playbook.

Ask about breach readiness

On-Call Advice for New Products, Features & Vendors

Whenever you launch something new that handles personal data, you can check with us first.

Where your IT team comes in: engineering and product teams can get advice before they release.

Ask about on-call advice

Significant Data Fiduciary Services

Large or high-risk data handlers can carry extra duties. We review the risks in algorithms and cross-border data transfers, and prepare evidence for the independent data auditor.

Where your IT team comes in: both the risk reviews and the audit evidence need technical input and records from IT.

Ask about SDF services

Regulatory Overlay

We map DPDP duties against RBI data localisation and outsourcing norms and SEBI cybersecurity and data governance circulars, so your controls do not clash or repeat.

Where your IT team comes in: the cybersecurity and data localisation parts directly affect how IT systems are set up.

Ask about regulatory overlay

Key dates

  • 13 November 2025: DPDP Rules notified
  • 13 November 2026: Consent Manager registration opens
  • 13 May 2027: full compliance required

Technology changes take time, so IT teams benefit from starting early.
